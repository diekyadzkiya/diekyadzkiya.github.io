# The Ultimate Beginner's Guide to Claude AI

## Level 1: Talk to Claude

### Chapter 1: What is Claude AI

Claude AI is an artificial intelligence assistant and family of large language models (LLMs) developed by Anthropic.

### Chapter 2: Create your free Claude account

Go to the [website](https://claude.ai/) and follow the registration process.

### Chapter 3: How to write good prompts

A good prompt consists of context, task and format.
+ context: who you are, what you're working with.
+ task: what you want.
+ format: how you want it, the length, the tone and the structure.
Don't start over when the answer isn't right, reply and refine.
